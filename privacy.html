Privacy Policy for RES A Reservations

Last updated: 27 November 2026

RES A Reservations is an AI-accessible restaurant reservation service that allows supported AI applications to check restaurant availability and create, retrieve, modify, or cancel reservations.

1. Information We Collect

When you use RES A Reservations to make or manage a reservation, we may collect information necessary to provide the reservation service, including:

  • Customer name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Reservation date and time
  • Party size
  • Reservation confirmation number
  • Reservation status

We may also collect limited technical information required to operate, secure, troubleshoot, and monitor the reservation service.

2. How We Use Information

Information collected through RES A Reservations is used to:

  • Check restaurant availability
  • Create restaurant reservations
  • Retrieve existing reservations
  • Modify reservations
  • Cancel reservations
  • Provide reservation confirmations
  • Prevent duplicate or fraudulent reservation requests
  • Maintain and troubleshoot the reservation service

3. AI Assistants and MCP

RES A Reservations may be accessed through supported AI assistants and applications using the Model Context Protocol (MCP).

When an AI assistant calls a RES A Reservations tool, information necessary to complete the requested reservation action may be sent between the AI application and the RES A Reservations service.

Users should review the privacy practices of the AI application they choose to use because information processed by that application may also be subject to the application's own privacy policy.

4. Information Sharing

Reservation information may be shared with the restaurant or its reservation system when necessary to perform a booking action requested by the user.

We do not sell customer personal information.

5. Data Storage and Retention

Reservation information may be stored for as long as reasonably necessary to operate the reservation service, maintain booking records, resolve problems, prevent abuse, and meet applicable legal or business requirements.

Data retention practices may be updated as the service moves from prototype testing to production use.

6. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information processed by RES A Reservations.

However, no internet-based system can guarantee absolute security. Users should not provide unnecessary sensitive personal information through the reservation service.

7. Prototype Status

RES A Reservations is currently under development and may be used with test or demonstration restaurant and reservation data.

The service should not be used to submit sensitive information, payment card information, government identification numbers, health information, passwords, or other information that is not required to create a restaurant reservation.

8. Payments

RES A Reservations does not currently collect or process payment card information as part of the prototype reservation service.

If payment or deposit functionality is introduced in the future, this privacy policy will be updated to explain the payment provider and how payment information is processed.

9. Your Choices

Users may contact us regarding questions about personal information associated with a reservation, including requests relating to correction or deletion where applicable.

10. Children

RES A Reservations is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children through the reservation service.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as RES A Reservations develops. The latest version will be published on this page with an updated revision date.
